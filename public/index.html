The idea behind AeroReads

A Better Way to Read More

Great books can inspire us, teach us, and take us to new worlds. But buying every book we want to read may not be practical.

AeroReads aims to make reading more accessible through book-rental plans that give readers another way to enjoy the books they love.

Our vision is simple: help readers explore more books with a plan that suits their reading habits and budget.

Why explore book rentals?

  • ✓Enjoy books without buying every title.
  • ✓Discover authors, stories, and new genres.
  • ✓Explore plans designed around different reading habits.
  • ✓Make more room for reading without owning every book.

Final plan prices are being confirmed. Request a callback to ask about the latest plan details.
